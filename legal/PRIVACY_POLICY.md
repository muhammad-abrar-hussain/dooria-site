## 3. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

### 3.1 Information you provide to us
- **Account & identity data:** your mobile phone number (used for sign-up and
  one-time-password login), and, if you choose to provide them, your name and
  e-mail address.
- **Delivery address data:** the delivery addresses you save, including street
  address, building/flat details, delivery notes, and their geographic
  coordinates.
- **Order data:** the items you order, add-ons, quantities, order notes, order
  history, and cancellation/refund records.
- **Reviews & feedback:** ratings, reviews, and complaints or support requests you
  submit.
- **Business/vendor applications:** if you apply to join Dooria as a business, the
  business information you submit (such as business name, owner name, e-mail, and
  phone).
- **Communications:** messages you send to our support channels.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically
- **Approximate and precise location data:** with your permission, we collect your
  device location to detect your current delivery address, show nearby
  restaurants, and support delivery. You can control location access through your
  device settings.
- **Device & technical data:** device type, operating system and version, app
  version, language, unique device or installation identifiers, IP address, and
  diagnostic/crash information.
- **Usage data:** how you interact with the Service (e.g. screens viewed,
  features used, searches made, recently viewed restaurants).

### 3.3 Information from third parties
- **Payment status:** where payment is processed by a third party or on delivery,
  we may receive confirmation of payment status (we do **not** store full card
  numbers — see Section 6).
- **Restaurant partners and delivery riders:** order-fulfilment and delivery
  status updates.

We do **not** knowingly collect special categories of data (such as religious,
political, health, or biometric data). Please do not submit such data to us.

## 6. Payments

Depending on the payment method, orders may be paid **on delivery** or through a
**third-party payment processor**. Where a third-party processor is used, your
payment-card details are collected and processed by that processor under its own
terms and privacy policy; we do **not** store full payment-card numbers on our
systems. We may retain limited transaction records (such as amount, date, and
payment status) for order management, accounting, and legal purposes.

We share personal data only as described below. **We do not sell your personal
data.**

  - **Google Maps Platform** (Google) — for maps, geocoding, and place search

- **Professional advisers** — lawyers, accountants, and auditors, where necessary.
- **Authorities and legal** — where required to comply with the law, a court
  order, or a lawful request, or to protect our rights, users, or the public,
  including under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 and other
  applicable Pakistani law.
- **Business transfers** — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or
  sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your device and account secure and
notify us promptly of any suspected unauthorised access.
